A little about me

Clear communication. Community-centered work.

I’m a Charleston-based communications professional with experience in journalism, digital content, and public service. I studied Communication and Media Studies at the College of Charleston, where I also contributed stories to The College Today. My work in a newsroom strengthened my ability to communicate clearly, meet tight deadlines, and tell stories in ways that connect with audiences.

Today, I support the work of a congressional district office, helping manage correspondence and coordinate with constituents and community partners. Across journalism and public service, I’m drawn to work that connects people with useful information, tells community stories thoughtfully, and makes complex subjects easier to understand.

Outside of work, running and yoga give me plenty to think about—and sometimes write about, too. This portfolio brings together the professional work I’ve done and the stories I hope to tell next.
